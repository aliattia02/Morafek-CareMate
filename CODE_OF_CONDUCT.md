# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Morafek CareMate community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in all discussions
- Giving and accepting useful, evidence-based feedback
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- Prioritizing patient safety, privacy, and security in all decisions
- Crediting others' work and contributions appropriately
- Welcoming contributors of all backgrounds and skill levels

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in any form
- Publishing private information (real names, addresses, patient data) without explicit permission
- Sharing exploit details or sensitive healthcare data in public channels
- Dismissing or ignoring security and privacy concerns raised by others
- Any conduct that could endanger users or compromise patient confidentiality

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, the project wiki, and any other public or private communication channel related to Morafek CareMate.

## Enforcement

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers via private GitHub message or open a private security report when the behavior involves sensitive data.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

## Consequences

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

1. **Warning** — A private or public warning with clarification of the violation.
2. **Temporary suspension** — Temporary ban from participation in the project.
3. **Permanent ban** — Permanent removal from all project spaces.
