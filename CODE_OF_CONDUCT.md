# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Morafek CareMate community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Giving and accepting useful feedback
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- Prioritizing patient safety, privacy, and security in discussions

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Sharing exploit details or sensitive healthcare data in public channels
- Any conduct that could endanger users or patient confidentiality

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and other public or private communication channels related to the project.

## Enforcement

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers via private channels or open a private security report when relevant.
